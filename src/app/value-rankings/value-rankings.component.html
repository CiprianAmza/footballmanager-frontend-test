Manager value sums each season and competition: (finish points + 40% of the difference from expected finish points + 80 points for a completed title) × competition reputation / 10,000 × score weight × the manager's share of club matches. A settled finish below the opening prediction instead loses 40% of the expected-result point gap, so that competition contributes negative value. Score weight is 70% for domestic cups, 30% for super cups and 100% for other competitions. The detailed title bonus already includes reputation, score weight and match share. Historic seasons without an opening prediction omit the expectation adjustment.

Player value sums goals and assists weighted by competition reputation / 10,000 and score weight, plus a share of the club result according to appearances. A settled finish below prediction gives the club result a negative value; individual goals and assists still earn points. Completed titles add 80 result points before these weights. Score weight is 70% for domestic cups, 30% for super cups and 100% for other competitions. Historic seasons without an opening snapshot omit the expectation adjustment.
